## Five principles applied

1. Imagery carries the layout: edge-to-edge hero, poster rows and no card shell around content.
2. Navigation stays quiet: horizontal text categories, underline library tabs and five compact bottom tabs.
3. Make returning to content direct: landscape progress cards; saved movie posters and vertical Shorts with title overlays.
4. Use typography, spacing and light dividers to separate sections; reserve borders for interactions and gold for membership/rewards.
5. Keep one clear action per area and honest status/data; account and benefits remain lightweight alongside content.
